1. WEIGHBRIDGE FLOW

When the truck arrives, the operator enters or scans the vehicle number.
Then the operator selects the farmer or supplier and the paddy variety.
The system records the gross weight of the loaded truck.
After that, the truck status becomes Inside or Unloading.
The system also prints the initial gate entry token.

Second is Truck Out.

When the truck is leaving, the operator selects the truck from the active list.
The system records the tare weight, which is the weight of the empty truck.
Then the system automatically calculates the net weight.

Net Weight equals Gross Weight minus Tare Weight.

If the moisture level is more than 14 percent, the system automatically applies the moisture deduction.
Finally, the system generates the final weighment slip and completes the gate pass with one click.


2. AUTOMATIC INVENTORY

When paddy comes into the mill, the raw paddy stock automatically increases.

When we process paddy in a milling batch, the system automatically deducts the raw paddy from stock.
At the same time, it adds the produced items to stock, such as head rice, broken rice, rice bran, and husk.

When we create a sales invoice, the system automatically reduces the sold rice bags from the warehouse.
The financial entries should also happen automatically.

For example, when we create a sales invoice, the system records the sales and the dealer's outstanding amount.
When a payment is received, the system updates the cash or bank balance and reduces the pending amount.


3. WHATSAPP AND PRINTING

We should add a simple Share on WhatsApp button to weighment slips and sales bills.
When the operator clicks it, WhatsApp Web or mobile should open with a ready-made message.

The message should contain important details such as the mill name, slip number, vehicle number, farmer name, net paddy, rate, and total amount.

For wholesale customers, we should also have a standard GST A4 invoice format.


4. DAY-END CLOSING

We should have one simple day-end closing screen.
For example, at 7 PM, the mill owner can open this screen and see the complete summary of the day.

It should show:
Total paddy received today.
Total number of trucks.
Total paddy quantity.
Total rice dispatched.
Total rice bags and metric tonnes.
Total cash and bank collections.
Total operational expenses.

Finally, it should show the net cash available in the mill.
The owner should be able to see all of this without opening multiple screens.


5. Part Account Book
We need a simple running ledger for farmers and dealers. The user should be able to search for any farmer or dealer by name.

The system should then show:
How much goods were delivered or purchased.
How much payment was made or received.
How much amount is still outstanding.

There should also be an option to export that to Excel or CSV. This will make it easier for the accountant and for GST-related work.
